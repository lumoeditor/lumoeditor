LUMO Camera 不要求创建账户。相机画面在设备上用于取景和拍摄；照片、滤镜、效果及导入的 LUT 在设备上处理。应用设置、收藏和应用内照片保存在应用本地存储中。LUMO Camera does not require an account. 2. 照片权限2. Photo Permissions

相机权限用于实时预览和拍摄。照片权限仅请求“添加照片”，用于您主动保存到系统照片图库；应用不会请求读取系统照片图库的权限。拒绝添加权限时，照片仍可保存在 LUMO Camera 的应用内相册。Camera access enables live preview and capture. Photo access is limited to “Add Photos” for saving images to Apple Photos when you choose; the app does not request permission to read your system photo library. If you decline, photos can still be kept in LUMO Camera’s in-app gallery.

The app has no server, account sync, or analytics service for uploading this content. You can delete photos in the app; uninstalling the app also removes its local data. Photos separately saved to Apple Photos must be managed in the Photos app.
