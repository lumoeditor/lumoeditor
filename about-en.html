About LUMO

Thank you for using LUMO.

I created LUMO to be a photo editor that is both simple and powerful—one that offers an immersive editing experience while giving your creativity room to shine.

Alongside familiar tools such as HSL, curves, and toning, LUMO offers powerful local adjustments. You can add, subtract, or intersect any mask to create complex visual effects.

LUMO lets you save edits as presets, so your color ideas are always within reach. You can also create versions of a photo and easily manage or switch between different looks.

Batch editing and batch export can greatly speed up your workflow. Watermark and layout tools also help you prepare finished work quickly.

I designed, developed, and tested LUMO entirely on my own. It may not be perfect, but I have put everything I can into making it the best experience possible.

If you have any suggestions for LUMO, please get in touch through “Contact Us” in the app.

I hope you enjoy the experience LUMO brings you.
