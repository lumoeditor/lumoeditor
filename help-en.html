Editing & Export

Why does my exported image have a watermark?

The watermark option was enabled when you exported the image. Turn off “Watermark” in the export panel to remove it from future exports.

Why is my exported image not actually 8K when I selected 8K export?

Some devices cannot fully process 8K images because of available memory and hardware limits. To avoid crashes or termination by the system, LUMO may automatically limit the maximum export resolution based on device performance.

Why do I not see the histogram after turning it on?

The histogram appears only when an editing panel is expanded. After enabling it, expand any editing panel to view the histogram.

Can I adjust the strength of a filter or LUT?

Yes. Select a filter or LUT, then tap the currently selected filter or LUT again to adjust its strength.

Does LUMO support RAW editing?

LUMO can currently read and edit RAW files, but it does not yet provide a complete high-bit-depth RAW processing workflow. As a result, it may not fully preserve all of the image quality and dynamic range of a RAW file. We plan to improve RAW support.

Why does the exported photo look slightly different from the editing preview?

Differences in screen display, color management, image format, and the way other apps render images can cause slight variations in color or brightness after export.

Why does exporting the same photo in 8K take much longer than in 4K?

Higher-resolution exports process more pixels and therefore use more memory and computing resources. The difference can be more noticeable when complex masks, filters, or local adjustments are applied.

Why does LUMO use more memory or make my device warm during export?

High-resolution processing, AI masks, and complex edits use more CPU, GPU, and memory resources. It is normal for a device to become temporarily warm while processing or exporting a large photo.

AI & Privacy

Does LUMO upload my photos?

No. LUMO processes and analyzes photos locally on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers or use them to train AI models.

Does LUMO modify my original photos?

No. LUMO uses non-destructive editing, so editing does not modify your original photo. A new image containing the edits is created only when you explicitly export it.

Why can’t some photos use the person, sky, or subject masks?

Person, sky, and subject masks rely on on-device image recognition. If the subject is very small, blurry, heavily obscured, or otherwise complex, it may not be recognized correctly or the mask may be less accurate.

Why do some AI masks take longer to generate?

AI masks are analyzed directly on your device, so processing time depends on device performance, photo resolution, and image complexity. Newer devices can usually complete the analysis faster.

Will my editing records remain after I delete LUMO?

LUMO projects are stored locally on your device. Deleting LUMO may also delete projects and editing records that have not been exported, so export important photos before deleting the app.

Subscriptions & Purchases

Why were premium features not unlocked after payment?

If your purchase was completed successfully but premium features are still locked, open LUMO’s Settings and tap “Restore Purchases.” Also make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account used to purchase LUMO.

How do I cancel my LUMO subscription?

LUMO subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → LUMO, then choose to cancel. You can continue using unlocked premium features until the current subscription period ends.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles refunds for purchases and subscriptions made through the App Store. Visit Apple’s “Report a Problem” page, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, select the relevant LUMO purchase, and submit a refund request. Apple decides whether a refund is approved.

Can I keep using my LUMO membership after changing devices?

Yes. Sign in to the new device with the same Apple Account used to purchase LUMO, then go to Settings → Restore Purchases to restore your subscription benefits.

Will restoring purchases charge me again?

No. Restore Purchases only checks existing purchases associated with your Apple Account; it does not create a new charge.
