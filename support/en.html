Camera & capture

CAMERA & CAPTURE
Why does the app ask for camera and photo permissions?

Camera access lets LUMO Cam show the viewfinder and take photos. Add Photos access lets it save finished images to your system photo library. You can change permissions anytime in iPhone Settings. Photos remain in the in-app library even if system library access is off.

Why is the viewfinder black or can’t I take a photo?

Check that camera access is enabled under Settings → LUMO Cam → Camera, then close other apps using the camera and try again. If the viewfinder is still blank, reopen LUMO Cam or switch cameras or lenses.

Why is RAW unavailable, or why was my photo taken in a standard format?

RAW availability depends on your iPhone model and the selected camera and lens. Some lenses do not support RAW, so LUMO Cam automatically takes a standard photo. Check RAW again after switching lenses.

How do I focus and adjust exposure?

Tap your subject in the viewfinder to set focus and metering, then use exposure compensation to fine-tune brightness. Behavior may vary slightly by iPhone lens and iOS version.

Why does my phone get warm or take longer to process photos?

RAW rendering and film looks require image processing. High resolution, continuous shooting, warm surroundings, or a low battery can increase processing time. Wait a moment, reduce bursts, and let the device cool before continuing.

Looks & LUTs

LOOKS & COLOR
Will the look I see in the viewfinder be applied to the photo?

Yes. The selected built-in camera look or imported LUT is used for the live preview and finished photo. RAW photos are rendered after capture, so the final result may look slightly different from the preview.

How do I import a LUT? Which formats are supported?

Open LUT in the camera menu, tap Import LUT, then choose a .cube or .lutbin file in Files. 3D LUTs from 2–128 points are supported, with input values from 0–1 and a maximum file size of 128 MB. Imported LUTs are selected automatically and stored on this device.

Why did my LUT import fail?

Make sure the file is a valid 3D LUT in .cube or .lutbin format, has 2–128 points, uses an input range of 0–1, and is no larger than 128 MB. If needed, export a standard 3D .cube file from your LUT editor.

Will my imported LUTs remain if I delete the app?

Imported LUTs are stored in LUMO Cam’s local app data. Deleting the app removes them. Back up the original LUT files in Files if you want to keep them.

Photos & saving

YOUR PHOTOS
Does LUMO Cam upload my photos?

No. Photos are captured and processed on your device and are not uploaded to the cloud. Subscriptions are handled by Apple StoreKit.

Where are my photos saved?

Photos are saved in LUMO Cam’s in-app library. If Add Photos access is enabled, finished images are also saved to your system photo library. You can browse, share, or save photos from the app.

What happens if I delete the app or a photo?

Deleting a photo in LUMO Cam removes it from the in-app library; copies already saved to your system photo library are not affected. Deleting the app removes photos and LUTs stored only in the app, so save or back up anything you want to keep.

Why can’t I save to my system photo library?

Allow Add Photos access under Settings → LUMO Cam → Photos, then try again. If saving to the system library fails, the finished image may still be in LUMO Cam’s in-app library.

Subscriptions & billing

LUMO CAMERA PLUS
What does a LUMO Camera Plus subscription include?

While your subscription is active, you can use all cameras and save photos. Monthly and annual auto-renewing plans are available. The App Store shows the price before you confirm a purchase.

My subscription status did not update after purchase. What should I do?

Make sure you are using the Apple Account used for the purchase, then wait briefly for the App Store to sync the transaction. In LUMO Cam, open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. If it still does not work, contact support with the approximate purchase time, device, and iOS version. Never send your password, verification code, or full payment details.

How do I cancel auto-renewal?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your Apple Account name, then Subscriptions. Select LUMO Camera Plus and cancel. You can keep using subscription features until the current paid period ends. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device or after reinstalling?

Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. In LUMO Cam, open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. If no active subscription appears, check your Apple Account and subscription status, then contact support.

Will restoring purchases charge me again? How do I request a refund?

Restore Purchases syncs your existing transaction with Apple; it does not make a new purchase or charge you again. Refunds are handled by Apple. Submit a request through your Apple purchase history or Apple Support.

Troubleshooting

A QUICK CHECK
The app stopped responding or is still processing a photo. What should I do?

High-resolution photos may take a little time to process. Keep the app in the foreground and wait. If nothing changes for a long time, close and reopen LUMO Cam. If it happens again, email us your iPhone model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue.

What information should I include when contacting support?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, LUMO Cam version, the steps before the issue, and what you expected to happen. Please do not attach personal or sensitive photos unless you are sure you want to share them.
