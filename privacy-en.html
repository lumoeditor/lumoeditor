Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

LUMO (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a photo editing application designed to provide powerful editing tools while respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use LUMO.

1. Photos and Editing Data

LUMO processes your photos and editing content locally on your device.

We do not upload, store, or access your photos, edited images, or editing projects on our servers.

LUMO only accesses your photo library when you grant permission, such as when you import photos for editing or save edited images.

Your photos remain under your control.

2. Image Processing Features

Some LUMO features use intelligent image processing technologies to provide editing capabilities such as photo adjustments, masking, and enhancement.

Image processing is performed locally on your device.

LUMO does not use your photos or editing content to identify individuals, create personal profiles, or train artificial intelligence models.

2.1 Face Data and On-Device Retouching

When you use portrait retouching or other face-aware editing tools, LUMO processes face regions in the photo you choose to edit. This may include detected face positions and boundaries, temporary image masks for regions such as skin, eyes, lips, teeth, and hair, and the retouching settings you choose for those regions. These data are used only to apply and restore your edits. Face labels identify regions within an editing project and are not used to identify a person across photos.

Face detection and image analysis run on your device using Apple Vision and machine-learning models bundled with LUMO. Photos, face positions, masks, and retouching data are not sent to LUMO servers or third-party AI services. They are not used for identity recognition, authentication, advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

Temporary analysis and rendering caches are held in app-process memory and may remain available across editing sessions until released or until the app process ends. To let you resume editing, LUMO also saves editing projects and settings in the app's local storage. These settings may include face-region positions and retouching values. Local project records do not have a fixed time-based expiration and may remain in app storage until replaced or the app is deleted. Deleting LUMO removes its local app storage; offloading the app may preserve that storage.

Photos and edits you export or save to your system Photos library are managed separately in that library and remain there until you delete them. Deleting LUMO does not delete items in Photos. Any iCloud Photos synchronization follows your device settings and is not a transfer to an AI service by LUMO.

3. Analytics Information

To improve LUMO and understand how users interact with the App, we collect limited usage analytics through PostHog.

This information may include general feature usage, editing activity, subscription-related events, and app interaction data.

We do not collect your photos, edited images, or editing results through analytics services.

4. Subscription Information

LUMO offers optional subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Payments and subscription management are handled by Apple. LUMO does not collect or store your payment card information.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID account settings.

5. Third-Party Services

LUMO uses third-party services to help us improve the App, including PostHog for product analytics.

These services do not receive your photos or edited content.

6. Data Security

Because your photos and editing projects are processed locally on your device, LUMO does not maintain a database of your personal photos or editing projects.

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will become effective when the updated version is made available.
