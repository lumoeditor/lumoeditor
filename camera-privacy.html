本政策说明 LUMO Camera 如何处理您使用应用时涉及的信息。This policy explains how LUMO Camera handles information involved in your use of the app.

1. 我们处理的信息1. Information We Process

LUMO Camera 不要求创建账户。相机画面在设备上用于取景和拍摄；照片、滤镜、效果及导入的 LUT 在设备上处理。应用设置、收藏和应用内照片保存在应用本地存储中。LUMO Camera does not require an account. The camera feed is used on your device for preview and capture; photos, filters, effects, and imported LUTs are processed on the device. App settings, favorites, and in-app photos are stored locally by the app.

2. 照片权限2. Photo Permissions

相机权限用于实时预览和拍摄。照片权限仅请求“添加照片”，用于您主动保存到系统照片图库；应用不会请求读取系统照片图库的权限。拒绝添加权限时，照片仍可保存在 LUMO Camera 的应用内相册。Camera access enables live preview and capture. Photo access is limited to “Add Photos” for saving images to Apple Photos when you choose; the app does not request permission to read your system photo library. If you decline, photos can still be kept in LUMO Camera’s in-app gallery.

3. 设备端存储与删除3. On-Device Storage and Deletion

照片、导入的 LUT、收藏和设置存储在本应用的设备沙盒中。本应用没有用于上传这些内容的服务器、账户同步或分析服务。您可以在应用内删除照片；卸载应用也会移除其本地数据。已另行保存到系统照片图库的照片需在照片应用中管理。Photos, imported LUTs, favorites, and settings are stored in this app’s device sandbox. The app has no server, account sync, or analytics service for uploading this content. You can delete photos in the app; uninstalling the app also removes its local data. Photos separately saved to Apple Photos must be managed in the Photos app.

4. 位置信息与照片元数据4. Location and Photo Metadata

应用不请求设备定位权限，也不主动收集您的精确位置。拍摄或保存的图像可能包含相机生成的技术元数据；元数据的保留情况可能因设备及系统照片服务而异。The app does not request device location access or actively collect your precise location. Captured or saved images may contain technical metadata generated by the camera; metadata retention can vary by device and Apple’s system photo services.

5. 第三方服务5. Third-Party Services

本应用不集成广告、分析、账户或云存储 SDK。若您使用 Apple 的系统照片保存功能，Apple 将按其隐私政策处理该操作。The app does not integrate advertising, analytics, account, or cloud-storage SDKs. If you use Apple’s system photo-saving feature, Apple handles that operation under its privacy policy.

6. 儿童隐私与安全6. Children’s Privacy and Security

本应用不专门面向儿童收集个人信息。请妥善保护设备并使用系统提供的安全功能；设备本地数据的安全也取决于设备的保护措施。The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. Protect your device and use its available security features; the security of local data also depends on your device’s protections.

7. 政策更新与联系7. Updates and Contact

我们可能因应用功能或法律要求更新本政策，并在本页面公布生效版本。有关隐私的问题，请联系 lumoeditor@outlook.com。We may update this policy to reflect app features or legal requirements and publish the current version on this page. For privacy questions, contact lumoeditor@outlook.com.
